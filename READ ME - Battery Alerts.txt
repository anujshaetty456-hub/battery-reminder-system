BATTERY ALERTS - HOW TO USE
============================

1. INSTALL: Double-click "Install.bat". A black window will flash and say
   "Done" - that's it, it's installed and already running.

2. HOW TO KNOW IT'S RUNNING: Look at the clock in the bottom-right corner
   of your screen. Click the little up-arrow (^) to show hidden icons -
   if you don't see anything, that's normal, it runs invisibly. You'll
   know it's working the moment you get your first alert.

3. WHAT YOU'LL SEE: A small pop-up message near your clock, with a sound,
   when your battery is FULL (unplug it), around 30% (plug in soon), or
   15% or below (plug in NOW). ALL THREE keep repeating - not just once -
   for as long as the problem isn't fixed:
     - FULL repeats every 2 minutes until you unplug the charger.
     - 30% repeats every 5 minutes until you plug in.
     - LOW (15% or below) repeats every 2 minutes until you plug in.
   Each one stops on its own the moment you fix it (unplug for FULL,
   plug in for 30%/LOW).

4. CHANGE THE ALERT LEVELS: Open "BatteryMonitor.ps1" with Notepad
   (right-click it > Open with > Notepad). Near the top you'll see:
       $FullThreshold = 95
       $MidThreshold  = 30
       $LowThreshold  = 15
       $FullRepeatSec = 120   (how often FULL repeats, in seconds)
       $MidRepeatSec  = 300   (how often 30% repeats, in seconds)
       $LowRepeatSec  = 120   (how often LOW repeats, in seconds)
   Change any number, save the file (Ctrl+S), then restart your PC
   (or double-click Install.bat again) for the new numbers to take effect.

5. REMOVE IT: Double-click "Uninstall.bat". It stops permanently and
   won't start again. Double-click "Install.bat" any time to bring it back.

No admin password or special permission is needed for any of this.
Nothing here uses the internet - it works with WiFi fully off.
